# PHASE 10 - ENTERPRISE POLISH

## OBJECTIVE

The platform is production-ready but must now achieve enterprise product quality.

The goal is to provide a polished, intuitive, and highly efficient user experience comparable to leading commercial DevOps platforms.

Target inspiration:

- Rancher
- OpenShift Console
- Datadog
- Grafana
- GitHub
- GitLab
- Portainer Business
- Backstage
- Lens IDE

Do not redesign the entire UI.

Improve consistency, usability, discoverability, and productivity.
